## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a vertical mouse?

A vertical mouse is an ergonomic computer mouse designed to keep your forearm in a neutral, upright position — similar to a handshake. Traditional mice force your forearm to rotate palm-down (a position called pronation), which compresses the structures in your wrist and can contribute to repetitive strain injuries over time. Vertical mice typically angle your hand between 50 and 70 degrees from the desk surface. Research suggests that reducing pronation decreases muscle activity in the forearm extensors — the muscles most associated with mouse-related discomfort.

### Who benefits most from a vertical mouse?

Anyone who uses a mouse for extended periods. The groups that see the most benefit are: **office workers** spending 4+ hours daily with a mouse, **people already experiencing wrist or forearm discomfort** during or after mouse use, **anyone recovering from repetitive strain** as part of an ergonomic workplace adjustment, and **remote workers** setting up long-term home office ergonomics. A vertical mouse is one component of an ergonomic setup — desk height, chair positioning, monitor placement, and break frequency all matter. If you are experiencing persistent pain, consult a healthcare professional.
